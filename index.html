About StockRoom

Notebooks and spreadsheets go out of date the moment someone takes an item. StockRoom keeps one live list of what you hold, where it is stored and who supplies it.

Each item has a reorder level. When the quantity falls to that level the item is marked low, so you order before the shelf is empty.

What StockRoom does

  • Record items

    Name, category, storage location, price and supplier in one place.

  • Track quantities

    Add or remove units with one click as stock arrives or is used.

  • Flag low stock

    Items at or below their reorder level are highlighted.

  • Value your stock

    See the total value of everything you hold, in UGX.

  • Search and filter

    Find an item by name or show only low or empty stock.

  • Supplier catalogue

    Browse sample products from a public catalogue and start a stock entry.
